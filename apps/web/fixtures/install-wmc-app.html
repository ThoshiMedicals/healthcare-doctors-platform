Patient app

Install the WMC Patient App

Add Woolloongabba Medical Centre to your home screen for faster access to the same patient tools you use on our website — booking, your preferred GP, Digital Reception, and practice contact details.

Need help now? Call reception on (07) 3217 3344.

What you get in the app

Phase 3A patient hub features, optimised for your phone.

  • Book appointmentsHotDoc online booking from your home screen.
  • Preferred GPKeep your GP choice handy on the patient dashboard.
  • Digital ReceptionMessage our team without waiting on hold when supported.
  • Call receptionOne-tap dialling to (07) 3217 3344.
  • Practice informationHours, location, and contact details aligned with our clinic profile.
