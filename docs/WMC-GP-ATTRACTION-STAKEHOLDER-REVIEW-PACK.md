## 1. Reviewer instructions

### What you are reviewing
Pages that help a visiting GP understand **this practice** — how the week feels, who patients are, whether they would fit, and how to get in touch. You are **not** reviewing the whole clinic website or the staff admin system.

### What “good” looks like
- Sounds like Woolloongabba Medical Centre, not a recruitment agency or software product  
- Feels honest about continuity, capacity, and fit  
- Imagery and stories feel believable for *this* rooms and *this* team  
- A cold GP would know what to do next without feeling pressured  

### Ground rules
1. Use the **live UAT site** only (facilitator will open the correct URL).  
2. Score **first impressions**, then note detail.  
3. Separate **“must fix before launch”** from **“nice later”**.  
4. Do not invent patient stories or name patients in feedback.  
5. If photography feels temporary or not “us,” say so plainly.  
6. Optional tools (readiness / DPA / provider numbers) are **not** the main story — flag only if they confuse or embarrass the practice.  

### Time
| Role | Solo walkthrough + scorecard | Group discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| External GP | 30–40 min | Optional |
| Internal (owner / PM / principal / nurse / reception) | 25–35 min | 20–30 min together |

## 2. Walkthrough script (facilitator)

**Opening (2 min)**  
“We’re reviewing how we present the practice to doctors who might join us. Please score as a careful colleague — not as a marketer. We want to know what must change before this goes on the public site.”

**Stop 1 — Join Our Team** (`/join-our-team.html`) · 4 min  
“Land here as if you were a GP who found us. Does the opening question and hero image make you curious about *this* practice? Note anything that feels like a product or brochure.”

**Stop 2 — Practice Experience** (`/about-us/practice-experience.html`) · 8 min  
“Skim Typical Day, Nursing, Reception, Team, Community, Continuity, Flexible Sessions. Does this describe our week? Where is it accurate, soft, or wrong? Do the photos feel like our rooms?”

**Stop 3 — Community & Catchment** (`/about-us/community-catchment.html`) · 4 min  
“Would a visiting GP understand who we look after and where we sit in Brisbane? Anything overclaimed or missing?”

**Stop 4 — Continuity (skim)** (`/about-us/continuity-in-practice.html`) · 2 min  
“Does continuity sound operational (same GP, recalls, notes) rather than slogan?”

**Stop 5 — GP Opportunities** (`/join-our-team/gp-opportunities.html`) · 5 min  
“Does ‘Would I fit here?’ feel fair? Are vacancy cards clear without overselling? Does the image support professional fit?”

**Stop 6 — Express Interest** (`/join-our-team/express-interest.html`) · 3 min  
“Would you be comfortable sending a colleague here to write to us? Anything awkward, corporate, or unclear?”

**Appendix (only if time / asked)** · 5 min  
Open one readiness tool. Ask: “Does this help or does it feel like an internal assessment system?”

**Close (1 min)**  
“Finish the scorecard and feedback form. We’ll consolidate must-change items next.”

## 5. Discussion prompts (group)

Facilitator: 20–30 minutes. Capture decisions in §7.

### Trust & authenticity
- If a respected GP peer landed on Join tonight, what would they trust — and what would they doubt?  
- Which image would you be comfortable showing as “this is us”? Which would you not?  

### Practice Experience
- Walk the Typical Day honesty cue: is it fair to high-throughput doctors?  
- Nursing and reception: anything we would not say in a real interview?  
- Flexible Sessions with little/no visual: does that chapter feel unfinished?  

### Continuity & community
- Are we claiming continuity we can deliver in rooms and booking habits?  
- Catchment: any suburb or patient mix we should add or soften?  

### Opportunities & contact
- Vacancy language vs fit language — balance right?  
- Express Interest: who in the practice reads it, and does the page set that expectation?  

### Tools
- Do readiness / DPA / provider tools belong on the public path, or only when a doctor asks?  

### Voices
- Whose consented voice should appear first (GP, nurse, reception, principal)? What must they *not* say?

## 6. Scoring framework

### Scale

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **5** | Launch-ready for this area — practice-true, clear, no material risk |
| **4** | Strong — minor polish only |
| **3** | Acceptable for UAT; **not** enough for production without a fix plan |
| **2** | Weak — confuses or misrepresents; must change |
| **1** | Unsafe / off-brand / blocks launch for this area |

### Area weights (for consolidation)

| Area | Weight | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trust | 15% | Reputation gate |
| Authenticity | 15% | “Is this us?” |
| Practice fit | 15% | Core GP job of the site |
| Continuity positioning | 10% | Brand promise |
| Community understanding | 10% | Place truth |
| Practice Experience | 15% | Flagship narrative |
| Opportunities | 10% | Conversion honesty |
| Readiness tools | 5% | Optional; high residue risk |
| Express Interest | 5% | Contact path |

**Weighted total** = Σ (score × weight) × 20 → report as **/100**.

### Banding

| Total | Band | Default stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 85–100 | Strong | Production Attraction GO *if* must-fix list empty |
| 70–84 | Conditional | Launch only after listed must-fixes |
| 55–69 | Hold | Re-review after photography/voices/ops fixes |
| &lt;55 | No-go | Do not promote Attraction to public production |

**Hard stops (any one overrides a high average):**  
- Material clinical or operational overclaim  
- Imagery stakeholders refuse to own as “us”  
- EOI path unclear or unstaffed  
- Continuity promised without booking/ops reality

## 8. Decision framework

### Question
**Can Attraction pages go to public production on the apex site?**  
(Separate from full DNS cutover / VPS ops — those stay on `deployment/LAUNCH.md`.)

### Decision table

| If… | Then… |
| --- | --- |
| Weighted mean ≥85 **and** must-fix list empty **and** no hard stop | **Attraction GO** for production index/publish planning |
| Weighted mean 70–84 **or** must-fixes ≤5 clear items | **HOLD — launch after must-fixes**; schedule re-score |
| Any hard stop **or** mean &lt;70 | **Attraction NO-GO**; keep UAT `noindex`; fix & re-review |
| Photography still concept-only but stakeholders accept temporary with written expiry | **Conditional HOLD**; written replacement date required |
