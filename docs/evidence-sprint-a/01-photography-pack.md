## E. Staff appearance guide

**Aim:** Ordinary working day, not corporate headshots.

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Usual clinical attire / scrubs / work clothes | Brand-new unused uniforms for the shoot only |
| Lanyards OK if no patient stickers facing camera | Stethoscope-as-prop posing |
| Natural hair/makeup as for clinic | Heavy glam / recruitment-agency look |
| Glasses on if usually worn | Looking straight down the lens for Critical shots |
| Conversational posture | Arms-crossed “hero team” lineup |

**Message to talent (read aloud):**  
“We’re documenting how the practice actually works for doctors who might join us. Keep doing your real work. Don’t smile at the camera unless it happens naturally.”
