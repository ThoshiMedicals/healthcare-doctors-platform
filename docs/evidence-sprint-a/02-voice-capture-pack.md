## C. GP interview guide (15–20 min)

**Open (2 min)**  
“We’re describing the practice for GPs who might join. We’ll only publish lines you approve. No patient details.”

**Prompts — ask 4; listen for one concrete sentence each**

1. What does a typical consulting day here feel like compared with elsewhere you’ve worked?  
2. How does nursing change what you can do in a session?  
3. What does continuity look like in the **book** — not the slogan?  
4. Who should **not** join us (fit honesty)?  
5. What would you tell a GP still deciding whether to write in?

**Close (2 min)**  
“We’ll email 1–2 sentences within 48 hours for your exact OK.”

**Target placements**

| Voice | Best lines from | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V-GP-01 | Q1 | PE Typical Day |
| V-GP-02 | Q2 | PE Nursing or Team |
| V-GP-03 | Q4 or Q5 | GP Opportunities |

**Avoid publishing:** income, guarantees, patient stories, competitor attacks.

---

## D. Nurse interview guide (15–20 min)

**Open:** Same consent / no patient identifiers.

**Prompts**

1. How do you protect GP consulting time on an ordinary day?  
2. Walk a real handoff (de-identified): flag → prep → GP join → recall close.  
3. What changes on a thinner nursing-cover day?  
4. What should a new GP understand about working with nursing here?

**Placements:** V-NU-01 → pathway (Q1/Q2) · V-NU-02 → cover honesty (Q3) on PE Nursing.

**Avoid:** Promising cover that isn’t rostered; procedure claims beyond scope.

---

## E. Reception interview guide (15–20 min)

**Open:** Same consent / screens and names stay private.

**Prompts**

1. How do you match appointment type to clinical need?  
2. What enquiries should **not** interrupt a consult — and how do you handle them?  
3. What does “running late” communication look like here?  
4. What do you wish GPs knew about front-of-house before they start?

**Placement:** V-RX-01 on PE Reception.

**Avoid:** Promising wait times you can’t keep; identifiable caller stories.

## F. Voice approval workflow

| Step | Owner | SLA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Quote consent signed | PM | Before interview |
| 2. Interview + recording/notes | PM or designated | Same day |
| 3. Extract 1–2 candidate lines (framework below) | PM / facilitator | **48 h** |
| 4. Email interviewee exact lines + attribution | PM | With step 3 |
| 5. Approve / edit / reject | Interviewee | **72 h** |
| 6. Principal skim (clinical risk only) | Principal | 24 h after approve |
| 7. Mark tracker Approved → hand to platform lead for UAT | PM | Same day |
| 8. Live check on UAT | PM | Before stakeholder walkthrough |

**No step 5 = no publish.** Empty slot beats a composite.
