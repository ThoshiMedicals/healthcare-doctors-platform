Three clear boundaries

Every support area below is organised the same way so you can see what the practice can and cannot do.

  • We can assist

    Practical orientation, location details, workflows, and team introductions — subject to capacity and mutual agreement.

  • We cannot approve

    Provider numbers, Medicare eligibility, visas, exemptions, or external credentials. Those are not practice decisions.

  • External agencies

    Services Australia, AHPRA, colleges, insurers, and others decide on their own rules and timelines.
