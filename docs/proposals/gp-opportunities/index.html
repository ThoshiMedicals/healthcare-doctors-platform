Practice philosophy

Care that knows your patients’ stories.

Woolloongabba Medical Centre is shaped by continuity, familiarity and long-term relationships. We look for GPs who want to practise medicine with context — not only episodic throughput. Clinical autonomy sits alongside respectful teamwork and AGPAL-aligned governance that supports safe care.

If that philosophy does not match how you prefer to work, this may not be the right practice — and that clarity is useful for everyone.

How we work

What to expect if you practise here.

Community care

We care for people across Woolloongabba and neighbouring inner-south suburbs. Community general practice here means everyday health needs, changing life stages and relationships that accumulate over years — close to where people live and work.

Facilities

Consulting environments are supported by nursing, reception and practice systems. The point of facilities is practical: rooms, clinical support and administration that let you focus on patients.

  • Consulting rooms suited to continuity-based general practice
  • Nursing support for treatment-room and preventive pathways where appropriate
  • Reception managing appointments, enquiries and patient flow
  • Policies aligned with AGPAL expectations for privacy, safety and quality

Flexibility

Permanent sessions, part-time arrangements and staged growth can be discussed. Flexibility at WMC means a clear agreement on days and reliability — for you, for patients and for the team — not a casual marketplace of leftover slots. Availability depends on rooms, nursing cover and practice need.

Clinical interests

Interests that strengthen community general practice are welcome when they fit local need, credentialing and team capacity — women’s health, skin checks, chronic disease, mental health, children’s health, procedural work and preventive care among them.

We remain a general practice first. Interests sit inside continuity care, not instead of it.

Professional collaboration

GPs practise with clinical autonomy within Australian standards and practice policy, alongside nurses, reception, practice management and allied health where relevant. Collaboration means colleagues you can ask, share cases with and co-manage complex presentations — without replacing your professional judgement.

Questions doctors ask

Decide with clear answers.

Is Woolloongabba Medical Centre focused on continuity of care? +

Yes. Our practice culture is built around long-term patient relationships, familiarity and care that can evolve over time — not high-volume throughput alone. GPs who value continuity tend to find the strongest fit here.

What support surrounds GP consulting? +

Nursing support assists with treatment-room activity, preventive pathways and follow-up where appropriate. Reception manages appointments and patient flow. Practice management and AGPAL-aligned systems support privacy, safety and everyday reliability.

Are flexible sessions possible? +

Permanent, part-time and staged session arrangements can be discussed. Flexibility means a clear agreement on days and reliability for patients and the team — confirmed individually against rooms, nursing cover and practice need.

Can I bring clinical interests into general practice here? +

Clinical interests that strengthen community general practice are welcome when they fit patient need, credentialing and team capacity. We remain a general practice first — interests sit inside continuity care, not instead of it.

Does the practice issue Medicare provider numbers? +

No. Medicare provider numbers are issued by Services Australia. Woolloongabba Medical Centre does not issue, approve or guarantee provider numbers. We can share accurate practice location details to support your application.

How do commercial arrangements work? +

Commercial terms are discussed privately with suitable doctors. We do not publish percentage splits or earnings claims on this page. Clarity before commitment matters more than marketing headlines.
