Why Woolloongabba Medical Centre

A professional home for community general practice.

We are a local practice shaped by continuity, familiarity and long-term relationships — not a high-volume clinic chasing throughput. GPs who join us become part of a multidisciplinary team that already cares for families across Woolloongabba and Brisbane’s inner south.

  • Continuity over churn

    Patient stories accumulate over years. Your clinical judgement builds on context, not first-visit fragments alone.

  • Team-based care

    GPs, nurses, allied health, reception and practice management work as one organisation around each patient.

  • Accredited standards

    AGPAL accreditation reflects structured quality, safety, privacy and continuous improvement in everyday practice.

  • Established community

    Inner-south location near major hospitals, with an established patient base and practical city access.

Facilities and support

The operational support that lets you focus on patients.

Good general practice depends on rooms, nursing, reception and systems that work. We invest in the environment around the consult — so clinical time is protected.

Consulting rooms & clinical space
Dedicated consulting environments suited to continuity-based general practice, with access to shared clinical areas as needed.
Nursing support
Practice nursing support for procedures, chronic disease pathways, immunisations and care coordination where clinically appropriate.
Reception & patient flow
An experienced reception team managing appointments, enquiries and the day-to-day experience patients associate with our practice.
Practice systems & governance
Structured policies for privacy, infection prevention, quality and safety — aligned with AGPAL expectations and local clinical reality.

GP FAQ

Questions GPs usually ask first.

Are you seeking permanent GPs, part-time GPs, or both? +

Both. We welcome vocationally registered GPs interested in permanent or flexible session arrangements that suit the practice and your clinical goals. Availability is discussed individually.

Do you support GPs who are building a patient list? +

Yes. Continuity is central to how we work. Reception pathways and team introductions help patients form an ongoing relationship with you — rather than treating every visit as a one-off.

What about billing models and commercial terms? +

Commercial arrangements are discussed privately with suitable candidates. This page does not publish percentage splits or earnings claims. Bring your questions — we will answer them directly in conversation.

Can I develop special clinical interests here? +

Interests that strengthen community general practice are welcome, subject to patient need, credentialing and team capacity. Share what you care about clinically in your expression of interest.

Do you accept GP registrars or non-VR doctors? +

Select the most accurate role in the form (including GP Registrar where relevant). Supervision capacity and timing vary — we will advise based on current practice needs.

How does recruitment work — and is this a job board? +

No. You submit an expression of interest directly to Woolloongabba Medical Centre. Our practice team reviews alignment with needs and timing, then contacts suitable candidates for a conversation. You are not added to a public job board or third-party talent pool.
