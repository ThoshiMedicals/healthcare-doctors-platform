GP Story Capture System

Operational layer for collecting and managing GP practice-experience stories: inventory, consent, status, categories, review, and content reuse controls.

Design rule. Human gates only — no auto-publish. Stories describe practice experience; they are not job ads, earnings claims, or agency assets. Default reuse is deny; channels are explicitly allowed per signed consent.
