**Total minimum:** **11 photographic slots** (or 10 + designed non-photo Flexible treatment) + **6 voices**.

Anything beyond this is Important / Nice-to-have — not required to cross the authenticity line.

## Privacy & consent rules (all collection)

- Written consent for every identifiable staff member (photo + quote use on public Attraction pages).  
- No identifiable patients on public Attraction imagery unless separate patient media consent exists (default: **no identifiable patients**).  
- Screens, charts, names, Medicare cards, DOB, address labels: blanked or out of frame.  
- Quotes: first name + role **or** role-only if preferred; no patient detail; no competitor-bashing; no income guarantees.  
- Prefer documentary / observational lighting over posed “recruitment smile” sets.

## 3. Doctor voice collection plan

### Critical quota
**3 GP voices** (minimum). Prefer: 1 principal or long-stay GP · 1 continuity-oriented GP · 1 part-time / flexible-sessions GP if available.

### Method
- 15–20 min interview (in person or call).  
- Record with consent; editor drafts 1–2 sentences; GP approves final wording + attribution.  
- No marketing rewrite that invents tone.

### Prompt bank (ask 4–5; publish 1–2 lines)

1. What does a typical consulting day here feel like compared with elsewhere you have worked?  
2. How does nursing change what you can do in a session?  
3. What does “continuity” look like in the book — not the slogan?  
4. Who should *not* join us (fit honesty)?  
5. What would you tell a GP still deciding whether to write in?

### Placement (frozen surfaces)
| Voice | Primary page | Secondary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GP–day | Practice Experience · Typical Day | Opportunities (optional) |
| GP–nursing/team | PE Nursing or Team | Opportunities |
| GP–fit / continuity | Opportunities and/or PE Continuity | FAQ only if already approved copy |

### Attribution options
`Dr [First] · GP` · `GP at Woolloongabba Medical Centre` · role-only if requested.
