# Staff briefing — Patient App install (reception)

**NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION publicly.** Internal use only until production cutover and QR verification.

## One-sentence pitch

“You can add Woolloongabba Medical Centre to your phone home screen for quicker booking, your preferred GP, Digital Reception and clinic details — it is not the HotDoc store app.”

## If the patient has Android

1. Ask them to scan the counter QR (or open the install link).
2. When the browser shows **Install** / **Add to Home screen**, help them tap it.
3. Confirm the icon appears on the home screen.

## If the patient has iPhone

1. The link must open in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Optional: if they see **Open as Web App**, they may turn it on.

## What not to say

- Do not claim it replaces HotDoc appointment reminders or recalls.
- Do not claim Digital Reception “messages the practice team” like SMS/chat staff.
- Do not promise push notifications until that programme is approved and launched.
- Do not display production QR materials while apex is still WordPress.

## Escalation

If install fails: continue with website / phone booking. Offer `(07) 3217 3344`.
