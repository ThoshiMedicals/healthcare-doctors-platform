Simple steps

Android

  1. Scan the QR code and open the link.
  2. When your browser offers Install or Add to Home screen, tap it.
  3. Confirm. Look for the WMC icon on your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Scan the QR code and open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an arrow up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
