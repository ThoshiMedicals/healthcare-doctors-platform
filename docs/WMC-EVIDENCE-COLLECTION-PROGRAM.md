## Consent process (all pillars)

### Forms (prepare before Sprint A day 1)
1. **Staff image consent** — use on public Attraction URLs listed; duration; withdrawal path  
2. **Staff quote consent** — approved wording only; attribution choice (name+role / role-only)  
3. **Patient media consent** — **default do not use**; if used, separate form + clinical lead sign-off  

### Process
1. PM issues forms ≥3 days before shoot/interview  
2. Signed PDF stored in practice secure folder (not in public repo)  
3. Asset library / CMS tag: `consent:yes` + expiry / review date  
4. Withdrawal: PM removes asset from Attraction slots within 5 business days  

### Privacy defaults
- Screens, charts, ID, Medicare, DOB out of frame or blanked  
- No identifiable patients unless form (2)+(3) complete  
- Quotes: no patient detail, no income guarantees, no competitor attacks

**GP:** day vs elsewhere · nursing change · continuity in the book · who should not join · message to a GP still deciding.
