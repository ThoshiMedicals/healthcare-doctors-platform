Save the Patient Hub on your iPhone

Safari does not show an automatic install button. Add this page to your Home Screen — it only takes a moment. This guide sits below the page heading so your dashboard stays visible.

  1. Stay in Safari on this page.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll the menu and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open the new icon on your Home Screen.
  5. If you see Open as Web App when adding, turn it on for a fuller screen experience (not on every iPhone).

Why save it?

  • Book appointments
  • Your Preferred GP
  • Digital Reception
  • Call the clinic
  • Practice information
