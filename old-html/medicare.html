At your appointment

Please bring your current Medicare card or Medicare number and any relevant concession card. We use this information to confirm billing eligibility and process eligible Medicare rebates electronically at reception where applicable.

Medicare and your appointment

Medicare is Australia’s universal health care system. If you are enrolled, Medicare may cover some or all of the cost of eligible health services. Bring your Medicare card or Medicare number to your appointment so we can confirm your details and billing eligibility.

Your Medicare card

If your card is lost, stolen, damaged or expired, Services Australia allows you to request a replacement through your Medicare online account linked to myGov or through the myGov app. A digital Medicare card can also be available through myGov.

If your address or contact details change, update them with Medicare and let reception know so we can keep your practice record current.

Medicare rebates

If a service is bulk billed, Medicare pays the provider directly and there is no Medicare rebate for you to claim separately for that service. If you pay privately for an eligible service, we may be able to submit an electronic Medicare claim on your behalf, or you can claim through Medicare.

Medicare questions

What Medicare information should I bring?

Bring your current Medicare card or Medicare number and any relevant concession card so we can confirm your details and billing eligibility.

What happens when a service is bulk billed?

When an eligible service is bulk billed, Medicare pays the provider directly and there is no separate Medicare rebate for you to claim for that service.
