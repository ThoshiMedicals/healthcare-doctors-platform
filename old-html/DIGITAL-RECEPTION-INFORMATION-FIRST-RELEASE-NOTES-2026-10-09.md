# Digital Reception - Information-first search refinements

## Scope
The approved website architecture, homepage, Knowledge Centre articles, service and practitioner pages, booking URLs and Lightbox controls remain unchanged.

- Clinical searches present all strongly matching Knowledge Centre article titles, descriptions and direct links, ranked by relevance. Five results appear initially; **Show more** reveals the next five without replacing or reordering existing results.
- Service and GP profile links are **not automatically inserted** in search results. Article pages retain their existing article-to-service-to-practitioner-to-booking pathways.
- Booking and Call Reception stay as separate persistent actions. Administrative searches such as parking, fees, test results and appointments still find their dedicated website pages.
- When no suitable guide is published (for example a general rash or unexplained breathing concern), the UI does not infer a diagnosis; it offers the Knowledge Centre directory and existing reception controls. Emergency wording still shows Triple Zero 000 guidance.
- The 194-page search corpus remains lazily loaded once per session, without a new external dependency.

## Test and release gates
`python cms/tools/check_digital_reception_coverage.py` verifies 49 patient-intent cases at desktop, 390px and 320px, with progressive disclosure, HotDoc controls, emergency safety, non-clinical navigation and lazy loading. Use `python tools/check_production_regression.py` and `python tools/check_knowledge_navigation.py` to verify page links, approved booking and the unified Knowledge Centre navigation.

This change has not been tested against live HotDoc provider network responses; final staging verification remains required. No medical advice is generated by Digital Reception.
