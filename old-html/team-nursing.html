Clinical support

Nursing support within general practice.

Our nursing team works alongside our doctors to support coordinated, patient-centred care. Nurses work alongside doctors in the day-to-day delivery of care and help patients move through appropriate clinical and preventive-health pathways.

How the team supports care

Practical clinical support around the consultation.

  • Support for treatment-room and general-practice clinical activities.
  • Support for preventive healthcare and vaccination pathways where appropriate.
  • Coordination with doctors when care requires ongoing follow-up.
  • Helping patients understand the practical next steps associated with their care.

Our nursing team supports day-to-day clinical care, follow-up and continuity across the practice.

Working together

Nursing support connects with the wider clinical team.

Nursing care does not sit separately from the rest of the practice. Nurses work within the clinical team and, where appropriate, support the practical steps around GP-led care, preventive health and follow-up. This helps patients move between parts of their care without losing sight of the overall plan.
