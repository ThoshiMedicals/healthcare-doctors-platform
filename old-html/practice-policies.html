A clear practice works better for everyone.

Our everyday policies help patients understand what to expect from appointments, communication, results, prescriptions, referrals, billing, privacy and follow-up.

Appointments.

Appointments are scheduled so clinicians can give appropriate time to each patient. If you have several concerns, complex paperwork or a matter that may need more time, tell reception when booking so the most suitable appointment can be discussed.

Booking information →

Cancellations and non-attendance.

If you need to cancel or change an appointment, contact us as early as possible so the time can be offered to another patient. Ask reception if you need information about any current non-attendance or cancellation fee.

Communication.

Reception can help with appointments and administrative information. Clinical advice, medication decisions and interpretation of results may require a consultation with a doctor. For urgent or worsening symptoms, seek timely medical advice rather than relying on routine messages.

Results, prescriptions and referrals.

We may ask you to book an appointment to discuss results or to obtain prescriptions and referrals. This allows your doctor to consider the request in the context of your current health and clinical needs.

Test Results→Repeat Scripts→Referrals→New Patient Prescribing Policy→AI-Assisted Consultation Notes & Consent→

Fees and billing.

Standard Medicare-eligible GP consultations follow our approved bulk-billing policy. Fees may apply to procedures, reports, employment or occupational medicals and other non-Medicare services. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical.

Fees & Billing→Bulk Billing→Medicare→

Privacy and respectful communication.

Patient information is handled confidentially, and respectful communication is expected across the practice. More detailed guidance is available in our privacy and patient-rights pages.

Confidentiality & Privacy→Patient Rights & Responsibilities→

Questions about a policy?

If you are unsure how a policy applies to your circumstances, contact reception before your appointment.
