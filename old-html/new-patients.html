Welcome to Woolloongabba Medical Centre

We welcome new patients to our practice. Whether you are new to Woolloongabba, Brisbane’s inner south or are looking for ongoing healthcare support, our team is committed to providing personalised, community-focused care with continuity over time.

Woolloongabba Medical Centre provides 100% Medicare bulk billing for standard Medicare-eligible general practice consultations for patients with a valid Medicare card. Fees may apply to procedures, reports, employment or occupational medicals and other non-Medicare services. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical.

Your first appointment

Your first visit is an opportunity for your practitioner to understand your health history, current concerns and what you need from ongoing care. Allow a little extra time for registration and for reception to confirm your details.

If you are unsure which appointment length is appropriate for the reason you need care, ask reception when you book.

What to bring

Please bring the information that will help reception and your practitioner prepare your record and understand your care needs.

  • Your Medicare card or Medicare number.
  • Your concession, pension or Commonwealth Seniors Health Card if applicable.
  • A current medication list.
  • Relevant medical records, referral letters or recent reports if available.
  • Photo identification if you have it available.

Medicare and billing

Bring a valid Medicare card so eligibility for bulk billed Medicare-eligible GP consultations can be confirmed. For more detail on Medicare pathways and when fees may apply, see Medicare, Bulk Billing and Fees & Billing.

After some appointments you may receive a HotDoc message asking you to complete Medicare Assignment of Benefit (bulk billing) consent. See Assignment of Benefit if you need an explanation of that process.

Booking your first visit

Appointments can be booked online through HotDoc or by contacting our reception team on (07) 3217 3344. Online booking lets you choose an available practitioner, appointment type and time. For a walkthrough of HotDoc booking, see Booking & HotDoc.

When you arrive

Please arrive a few minutes early for your first visit so reception can complete registration. Tell reception if you need communication support, accessibility assistance or have arrived late.

Parking is available nearby, including disability parking, and our medical centre is designed for wheelchair access. If you have specific access needs, contact reception before attending.

Patient questions

New patient questions

How do I book my first visit?

Book through HotDoc or call reception on (07) 3217 3344. Reception can help if you are unsure which appointment length is appropriate.

Book Appointment

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring your Medicare card or number, any concession cards, a current medication list and relevant records or referrals if you have them. Photo identification is helpful when available.

How does bulk billing work for new patients?

Woolloongabba Medical Centre provides 100% Medicare bulk billing for standard Medicare-eligible general practice consultations for patients with a valid Medicare card. Fees may apply to procedures, reports, employment or occupational medicals and other non-Medicare services. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical.

What time should I arrive?

Arrive a few minutes early for your first visit so reception can complete registration and confirm your details before your consultation.

Can I contact reception before my first visit if I need accessibility support?

Yes. If you have mobility, communication or other access needs, contact reception before attending so the team can explain available arrangements.
