How results are managed

Our current policy is that test results are discussed with a doctor so they can be interpreted in the context of your health and the reason the test was requested. If your usual doctor is unavailable, another doctor practising at the clinic can review the result with you.

Results are not simply administrative information; they may need interpretation alongside your symptoms, history, medications and the reason the test was requested.

Patient communications

We use HotDoc to help us reach you.

Woolloongabba Medical Centre uses HotDoc as a patient communication partner. Depending on the clinical context and our current settings, you may receive a secure health or result message by SMS or email that asks you to verify your details before viewing the message or booking follow-up care.

A HotDoc message does not replace clinical review. If your doctor asked you to return, or you are worried about a result or your symptoms, book the recommended follow-up or contact the clinic.

Follow-up

Book a follow-up appointment after your test when your doctor has advised review is required, or whenever you are uncertain about the result or next step.

We also use recall and reminder systems to support preventive and follow-up care. Please keep your phone number, email and address current so the team can contact you when needed.

Significant or urgent findings

If a result requires prompt clinical attention, we may contact you using the details held in your record. The absence of a call should not be taken as confirmation that a result is normal or requires no follow-up.

Your responsibility

If you were asked to return for results, please make the follow-up appointment even if we have not contacted you.

Recall and reminder system

We use a recall and reminder system to support preventive care and follow-up. From time to time the team may contact you about care that is appropriate for you. Please keep your contact details current and discuss any questions about reminders with your doctor.

The current appointment guide lists a simple results follow-up as a standard consultation.
