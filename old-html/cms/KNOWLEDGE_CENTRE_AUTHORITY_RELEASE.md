# Healthcare Knowledge Centre Authority Content — Editorial Release Candidate

This release contains 42 patient education pages (29 expanded existing guides; 13 fully authored new guides). It preserves the approved institutional website design, doctor biographies, booking functionality and branding.

**Not a record of clinical approval or live publication.** All 42 changed pages need named clinical reviewer approval of the final page SHA-256, SEO/accessibility approval, and management sign-off **before deployment**. No clinical reviewer, publication date or next review date has been invented. Register approvals and deployment evidence through the existing governance workflow.

## Release steps

1. Assign content owner and practising-clinician reviewer; verify clinical accuracy, warnings, current Australian vaccination/screening advice, external source links, availability of services and practitioner relevance.
2. Confirm article titles, canonical URLs, accessibility and human editorial quality. Confirm any superseded clinical terminology (especially GP chronic condition management plans).
3. Record approval evidence against each `sha256` in `cms/knowledge-centre-authority-review-queue.json` and existing governance records. Recalculate after any page edits; previous approval is invalid after changing bytes.
4. Only after sign-off: deploy the website, verify each live page, confirm HotDoc booking and internal links, and confirm indexing and sitemap submission.
5. Record actual publication dates and set clinically appropriate review dates; review earlier if government guidance changes.

## Treatment of existing published material

The prior 29 article URLs were preserved to protect existing citations and search equity. Their expanded texts are new draft revisions and require re-approval, even though the old versions were previously published. The 13 new article slugs are original draft content; no claim of live publication has been made.

## Clinical content safeguards

General educational information only. Emergency symptoms direct to 000. No individual medical advice, no guaranteed Medicare billing eligibility or specific doctor availability. Practitioner names and areas of care were mapped to the verified profiles in this supplied website. The 2026 Australian immunisation guidance and 2025 GP chronic-condition plan rules require periodic rechecking.
