Introduction

The best appointment is the one that allows safe assessment and practical follow-up. Telehealth can suit selected conversations and existing-care reviews; face-to-face appointments allow your GP to examine you, check observations and perform necessary tests.

Why This Topic Matters

Convenience is useful, but it should not delay diagnosis. A symptom that sounds straightforward may need physical assessment. It is normal for a telehealth clinician to ask you to come in if the information available remotely is insufficient.

Signs, Symptoms or Situations Relevant to Patients

The following situations are worth recognising. They do not establish a diagnosis by themselves:

  • Telehealth may suit routine results, selected mental health conversations and follow-up agreed with your GP.
  • Face-to-face care is often preferable for new abdominal pain, persistent cough needing chest examination, a changing skin lesion or a child who is acutely unwell.
  • Injuries, injections, cervical screening and most procedures require attendance.

When Should I Seek Medical Advice?

Describe your symptoms accurately when booking and say if they are worsening. Severe chest pain, serious breathing trouble, significant bleeding or stroke-like symptoms require 000, not either routine appointment format.

How Can a GP Help?

The practice can help you choose an appointment, and the GP may change that recommendation after initial assessment. Your clinician can explain whether Medicare telehealth eligibility applies, what fees may arise, and whether further tests or follow-up are required. Patients from Woolloongabba and nearby Brisbane inner-south communities can discuss the most appropriate next step with the practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which appointment is better for a skin check?

An in-person examination is required to properly examine a suspicious skin lesion.

Can mental health appointments be by telehealth?

They can be suitable in some cases, depending on clinical needs, preferences and eligibility.

Does telehealth save me a referral visit?

Sometimes, but referrals still depend on clinical assessment and an examination may be needed first.
