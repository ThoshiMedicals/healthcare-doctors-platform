Introduction

Heart health involves more than an ECG or cholesterol number. A preventive GP assessment considers multiple risk factors together.

What Is It?

An Australian Heart Health Check is a structured cardiovascular risk assessment for eligible people without known cardiovascular disease. It may include age, family history, smoking, diabetes, blood pressure and cholesterol, and a discussion of future heart attack or stroke risk.

  • Family history of heart disease
  • Questions about five-year cardiovascular risk
  • Blood pressure, diabetes or cholesterol concerns

Should I Be Concerned?

Talk to a GP if you are approaching the age for preventive checks, have a strong family history or are worried about diabetes, cholesterol or blood pressure. Heart Health Check Medicare eligibility depends on current criteria and your health history.

When Should I Seek Medical Advice?

Chest discomfort that persists or spreads to an arm, jaw or back, sudden breathlessness, collapse or stroke signs require a call to 000. A preventive assessment is not intended for evaluating possible acute heart attacks.

Urgent symptoms: If this is a life-threatening emergency, call 000. Do not use the booking form for emergency care.

How Can a GP Help?

Your GP can assess cardiovascular risk and discuss a realistic prevention plan: eating patterns, activity, smoking cessation, medicine where appropriate and scheduled follow-up. Not every patient requires every test.

Bring home blood-pressure readings, recent test results and an up-to-date list of prescribed and over-the-counter medicines. If unsure which appointment to choose, call reception on (07) 3217 3344.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Heart Health Check only a blood test?

No. It brings together several risk factors and a discussion of prevention goals.

Does Medicare cover heart health checks?

There are Medicare items for eligible patients, but eligibility and any fees must be checked for your appointment.
