Understanding Telehealth & Access To Care

Access to care includes knowing when telehealth is suitable, when an in-person examination is important and how to navigate local services. General practice can help patients choose the safest and most practical pathway.

Our practice supports healthcare access for Woolloongabba and nearby communities, with in-person and telehealth options where clinically appropriate.
