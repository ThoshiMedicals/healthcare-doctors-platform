Understanding Children's Health

Children's health needs change quickly with age and development. General practice supports preventive care, vaccinations, assessment of common illnesses and continuity for children and families over time.

How to use this hub

Education pathway

  • What this covers — Children's GP care including common illness, growth concerns, developmental questions and vaccination pathways.
  • When to seek care — Book for persistent symptoms, fever concerns, developmental questions, or scheduled child health and vaccine visits.
  • What a GP can do — A GP assesses the child in context, advises next steps, and coordinates vaccination or referral when needed.
  • What to bring — Immunisation history, growth notes if available, and a clear description of current concerns.
  • Trusted information — Australian childhood health and immunisation resources support this hub.

Frequently asked questions

When should I take my child to a GP versus urgent care?

Book a GP review for ongoing concerns, rashes, mild fever with otherwise well appearance, or developmental questions. Seek urgent/emergency care for severe breathing difficulty, unresponsiveness, stiff neck with fever, or rapidly worsening illness — call 000 if life-threatening.

How do I prepare for my child’s first GP visit?

Bring the child’s health record, immunisation history, medicines and a brief symptom timeline. See our patient guide on preparing for a first visit.

Can I request a longer appointment for several child health issues?

Yes — tell reception if you need developmental, behavioural, or multi-issue discussion so enough time can be allocated.

Where do childhood vaccinations fit?

Vaccination schedules are covered in our vaccinations hub and children’s guides. Book via Children’s Health or Vaccinations.

When should I seek urgent care for a child?

Seek urgent or emergency care for breathing difficulty, unresponsiveness, a non-blanching rash with illness, or other severe warning signs. Call 000 when needed.

When should I book a GP for my child?

Book for persistent fever or illness, feeding or growth concerns, developmental questions, or scheduled health and vaccination visits.

When should I ask for a longer children's appointment?

Ask for more time for developmental reviews, multiple concerns, or complex history.

What should I bring to a children's visit?

Bring immunisation history, growth notes if available, and a clear list of current concerns.
