Preventive healthcare is easier when follow-up is planned.

We use reminders and recalls to support preventive healthcare and clinically important follow-up. Depending on your circumstances, we may contact you when a vaccination, screening activity, review or other follow-up may be due.

Patient communications

Reminders and recalls may be sent through HotDoc.

We use HotDoc as a patient communication partner for appointment reminders, preventive-health recalls and other follow-up messages. These communications help us prompt patients when care may be due, but they do not replace the follow-up plan your doctor has given you.

Keep your mobile number and email address current so important practice communications can reach you.

What is the difference between a reminder and a recall?

A reminder may let you know that routine preventive care, such as a vaccination or screening activity, may be due. A recall may be used when your treating clinician or the practice needs you to return for clinically important follow-up. The exact reason will depend on your care.

What the service may cover.

  • Vaccinations and immunisations.
  • Screening and preventive health activities.
  • Follow-up requested by your treating clinician.
  • Ongoing monitoring where follow-up forms part of your care plan.

Keep your contact details current.

Reminder and recall systems work best when we have your current contact information. Please tell reception if your phone number, address or other important contact details change.

Do not wait for a reminder if you have already been given a plan.

If your doctor asks you to return, arrange a test, review a result or seek care if symptoms change, follow those instructions even if you have not received a reminder or recall. If you are unsure what happens next, contact us.
