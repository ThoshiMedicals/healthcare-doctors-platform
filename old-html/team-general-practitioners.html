Working together

The GP is often the clinical anchor of the team.

General practice often sits at the centre of a patient’s healthcare. Your GP can bring together symptoms, history, medicines, results and information from other health professionals, then coordinate nursing support, allied health, referrals and follow-up where appropriate. This is one reason continuity with a familiar GP can be valuable over time.
