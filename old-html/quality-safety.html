Why quality matters.

Quality healthcare depends on more than a consultation. It also depends on the systems around care: clinical governance, privacy, infection prevention, safe processes, clear communication, patient feedback and continuous improvement.

What quality and safety look like in practice.

Patient safetySystems designed to reduce avoidable risk and support consistent care.
Clinical governanceClear responsibilities, policies and processes supporting safe healthcare.
PrivacyConfidential and appropriate handling of personal health information.
Infection preventionRoutine systems supporting safe clinical environments, equipment and procedures.
Quality assuranceStructured review of systems, risks, feedback and practice processes.
Continuous improvementUsing review, evidence and feedback to identify where care systems can be strengthened.

Why AGPAL accreditation matters.

AGPAL accreditation provides independent assessment against the applicable national general practice standards. For patients, the important point is not the badge itself—it is the external framework behind it, which looks at the systems that support safe, organised and quality-focused general practice.

Quality belongs to the whole practice.

Day to day, safe and dependable care depends on the whole team. Clinicians, nurses, management and reception staff each contribute to the systems patients rely on before, during and after an appointment.

Why Team-Based Care Matters →

Patients are part of quality improvement.

Questions, feedback and complaints can identify where communication or systems need attention. Patients should be able to understand how care works, raise concerns and know where to find independent support if needed.
