AI-Assisted Consultation Notes & Consent

Some doctors may use AI-assisted transcription to help prepare consultation notes. It is used only with your consent and never replaces your doctor’s clinical judgement.

What this technology does

AI-assisted transcription, sometimes called an AI scribe, can listen to a consultation and help prepare a draft transcript or clinical note. It is a documentation aid. It does not diagnose you, decide your treatment or replace the conversation between you and your doctor.

Your consent comes first

Before an AI-assisted transcription service is used, your doctor will explain that it is being proposed and ask for your consent. You can say no. Declining does not affect your ability to receive care at Woolloongabba Medical Centre.

What may happen during the consultation

Depending on the service being used, consultation audio may be processed to create a draft transcript or note. Your doctor remains responsible for checking the information, correcting it where needed and finalising the clinical record.

Privacy and health information

Health information is sensitive. We expect AI-assisted documentation tools used in the practice to be considered within our privacy, security and clinical-governance processes. You may ask your doctor which service is being used and what information it processes. We do not make provider-specific storage or retention claims on this page unless they have been verified for the service in use.

You can change your mind

If you consent and then become uncomfortable, tell your doctor. They can stop using the transcription service where practicable and continue the consultation without it.

Human review remains essential

AI-generated transcripts and draft notes can be incomplete or inaccurate. Your doctor reviews the output and remains responsible for the clinical record and the care decisions made during your consultation.

Questions patients often ask

Do I have to agree to AI-assisted transcription?

No. You can decline. Your consultation and access to care can continue without the transcription service.

Does the AI make clinical decisions?

No. The technology assists with documentation. Your doctor remains responsible for clinical judgement, decisions and the final medical record.

Can I ask which AI service is being used?

Yes. You can ask your doctor what service is being used and how it is intended to support documentation during your consultation.

What if I change my mind after I consent?

Tell your doctor. They can stop using the transcription service where practicable and continue the consultation without it.

Where can I read more about privacy?

Read our Confidentiality & Privacy information or speak with the Practice Manager if you have a question about how your information is handled.
