Your information deserves careful protection.

Your health information is private.

Your medical record contains personal and health information that is important to your care. We treat that information as confidential and limit access to authorised people who need it for legitimate healthcare or practice purposes.

What may be held in your record.

Your record can include identification and contact details, Medicare information, medical history, medications, allergies, consultation notes, results, referrals and other information needed to provide or coordinate your healthcare.

How information is used and shared.

Health information is used to provide and coordinate your care and to support the safe operation of the practice. Information may also need to be shared when you consent, when another healthcare provider needs information for your care, or when disclosure is otherwise authorised or required. We aim to share only what is reasonably needed for the purpose.

Accessing or correcting your information.

You can ask about information held in your record and request access to your health information. If you believe information is inaccurate, you can ask us to correct it or record your concern. Some limits can apply where disclosure would affect another person’s privacy or create a serious risk.

Keeping information secure.

Privacy depends on everyday systems as well as professional confidentiality. Access controls, staff responsibilities, secure clinical systems and appropriate handling of documents and messages all contribute to protecting patient information.

Patient communications

HotDoc is one of our patient communication partners.

We use HotDoc to support patient communications such as appointment reminders, recall and result messages, digital forms, review requests and payment requests. HotDoc provides the communication platform; questions about your healthcare, results, billing or records should still be directed to Woolloongabba Medical Centre.

We also use a separate, consent-based AI-assisted transcription process in some consultations. You can read how consent and clinician oversight work on our AI-Assisted Consultation Notes page.

Digital Reception and website questions.

Digital Reception is designed to help visitors find information already published on this website. The production version uses a local website-content index and does not access your medical record or search the public internet. Please do not enter personal, clinical or sensitive health information into Digital Reception.

Privacy questions or concerns.

If you have a privacy question, want to request access to information, believe something is inaccurate or want to raise a concern about how information has been handled, contact the practice and ask to speak with the Practice Manager.
