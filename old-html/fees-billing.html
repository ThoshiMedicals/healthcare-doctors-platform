Medicare billing at Woolloongabba Medical Centre

Woolloongabba Medical Centre provides 100% Medicare bulk billing for standard Medicare-eligible general practice consultations for patients with a valid Medicare card.

Billing pathways

Find the billing information that applies to you.

Use these pages together, or call reception before your appointment if you are unsure whether a fee may apply.

Bulk Billing→Medicare Exclusions→Ask reception about fees→

When fees may apply

Fees may apply to procedures, reports, employment or occupational medicals and other non-Medicare services. That includes a WorkCover or work injury medical, and insurance medicals or insurance forms prepared for an insurer or another third party. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical.

Patient communications

Payment requests may be sent through HotDoc.

Where a payment is required, Woolloongabba Medical Centre may use HotDoc to send a payment request by SMS, email or app notification. The request relates to a fee, appointment or service arranged with the practice.

If you are unsure about a payment request, contact reception before entering payment details. Billing questions remain with Woolloongabba Medical Centre.

Before your appointment

Bring your current Medicare card and any relevant concession or healthcare information. If you are booking a procedure, report, employment medical or another service that may sit outside Medicare, reception can explain the expected fee before the appointment.

Billing questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are standard GP consultations bulk billed?

Woolloongabba Medical Centre provides 100% Medicare bulk billing for standard Medicare-eligible general practice consultations for patients with a valid Medicare card.

Can procedures have an additional fee?

Fees may apply to procedures, reports, employment or occupational medicals and other non-Medicare services. That includes a WorkCover or work injury medical, and insurance medicals or insurance forms prepared for an insurer or another third party. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical.

Are reports and employment medicals covered by Medicare?

Reports, employment or occupational medicals — including a WorkCover or work injury medical — and insurance medicals or insurance forms may be privately billed because they may not attract a Medicare benefit.

Will I be told before a fee is charged?

Yes. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical so the expected cost is clear before treatment.
