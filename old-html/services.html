Care that grows with you.

Our services are designed to support patients through every stage of life, from childhood and family healthcare through to preventive care, chronic disease management and healthy ageing.

Whether you are seeking routine healthcare, support for an ongoing condition or advice about your wellbeing, our team is committed to providing community-focused care that grows with you.

Family HealthOngoing healthcare for individuals and families of all ages, including acute illness, preventive care, health assessments and long-term health planning.→Women's HealthSupport through changing healthcare needs, including preventive screening, reproductive health, pregnancy care, menopause and general wellbeing.→Men's HealthPreventive and ongoing healthcare including general health assessments, cardiovascular risk review, prostate health discussions and healthy ageing.→Children's HealthHealthcare for babies, children and adolescents, including vaccinations, developmental monitoring, common childhood illness and preventive care.→Mental HealthPrimary healthcare support for anxiety, depression, stress, burnout and mental health concerns, with care plans and referrals where appropriate.→Chronic Disease CareContinuity of care and coordinated planning for long-term conditions such as diabetes, heart disease, asthma, COPD, arthritis and complex health needs.→Preventive HealthcareHealth assessments, risk reviews, screening discussions and lifestyle support designed to identify concerns early and support long-term wellbeing.→VaccinationsVaccination planning for children and adults, including routine immunisation, seasonal vaccination and travel-related vaccine discussions.→Skin ChecksAssessment of moles, spots and skin changes, with advice about monitoring, treatment or referral where appropriate.→Medical ProceduresInformation about procedures and assessments including iron infusions, ECG, spirometry, driver medicals, contraceptive procedures and cervical screening.→Allied HealthChiropractic and physiotherapy pathways that can work alongside general practice as part of connected care.→TelehealthConvenient access to selected healthcare needs, including follow-up care, medication reviews, results discussions and planning where clinically suitable.→

Independent providers

Other services patients can access on site.

Pathology, radiology and pharmacy services are available through independent third-party providers. These providers are separate businesses and are not owned or operated by Woolloongabba Medical Centre. Their availability, opening hours, fees, billing and service conditions are set by the individual provider.

PathologyIndependent on-site pathology collection access for requested tests and specimen collection.→RadiologyIndependent on-site diagnostic imaging access; available imaging types and bookings are provider-specific.→PharmacyIndependent pharmacy access for prescriptions, medicines and pharmacist advice.→

Continuity of care

Healthcare works best when it is connected.

Our approach combines continuity of care, preventive healthcare and long-term health management to support the wellbeing of our community. By developing an understanding of each patient's history, goals and circumstances, we aim to provide care that remains consistent and personalised over time.
