After-hours care when we are closed

Know where to seek help after hours and when emergency care is required.

By Practice Team

After-hours medical help

When we are closed, patients can contact Hello Home Doctor on 134 100 or book online. It provides after-hours GP home visits and, where clinically appropriate, telehealth. Published consultation hours are Monday to Friday 6:00 PM–8:00 AM, Saturday from midday, and Sunday/public holidays 24 hours. Eligibility and coverage conditions apply.

Health advice in Queensland

13 HEALTH is available on 13 43 25 84 for health assessment and advice from registered nurses.

Emergency care

For a life-threatening illness or injury, call Triple Zero (000) and ask for an ambulance.
