Appointments

How do I book an appointment?

Appointments can be booked online through HotDoc or by calling reception on (07) 3217 3344.

Book an appointment →

When should I consider a longer appointment?

A longer appointment may be helpful if you have several concerns, a complex health history, a mental health discussion or forms that require clinical review. If you are unsure, ask reception about the available appointment types.

Booking information →

What if I need to change or cancel an appointment?

Contact reception as early as possible if your plans change so the appointment can be released for another patient.

Appointment options →

What if I am not sure which appointment type to choose?

Reception can explain the available appointment types and appointment lengths. Clinical decisions about your care are made by your practitioner.

New Patients

Are new patients welcome?

Yes. Woolloongabba Medical Centre welcomes new patients and families. The New Patients page explains how to prepare for a first visit.

New patient information →

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring your Medicare card or Medicare details, any relevant concession card, an up-to-date medication list and relevant medical information such as recent letters, reports or results if available.

What to bring →

Can members of the same family attend the practice?

Yes. Many families choose the same practice for convenience and continuity, while each person’s healthcare information and privacy remain individual.

Family Health →

How can I prepare if I have a longer or more complex medical history?

Bring a current medication list and any recent specialist letters, reports or results that may help your practitioner understand your care. Your practitioner can advise if any additional information is needed.

Fees & Medicare

Do you bulk bill?

Woolloongabba Medical Centre provides 100% Medicare bulk billing for standard Medicare-eligible general practice consultations for patients with a valid Medicare card.

Bulk billing information →

Can some services still have a fee?

Yes. Fees may apply to procedures, medical reports, employment or occupational medicals and other non-Medicare services. Where a fee applies, patients are advised in advance wherever practical.

Fees & billing →

What should I bring for Medicare?

Bring your current Medicare card or Medicare details and any relevant concession information.

Medicare information →

How can I check whether a fee applies before my appointment?

Review the Fees & Billing information or contact reception before your appointment. Where a fee applies, the practice aims to explain it in advance wherever practical.

Fees & billing →

Results & Scripts

How are test results managed?

Follow the instructions given by your doctor or our team. Some results require a consultation so they can be interpreted in the context of your health and the next steps can be discussed.

Test results information →

What should I do if I am worried about a result?

If you are concerned about a result or your symptoms are changing, contact the practice for advice rather than waiting for a routine follow-up. For a life-threatening emergency, call Triple Zero (000).

Test results information →

Can I request a repeat prescription without an appointment?

Prescription requirements depend on the medication and your clinical circumstances. A doctor may require a consultation or review before prescribing.

Repeat prescription information →

Why might my doctor ask to review me before issuing a prescription?

Safe prescribing may require an up-to-date review of your condition, medicines, monitoring or other clinical information. The required follow-up depends on your individual circumstances and the medicine involved.

Repeat prescription information →

Telehealth

Do you provide telehealth appointments?

Telehealth may be available for selected healthcare needs where a remote consultation is clinically suitable.

Telehealth information →

Can every health concern be managed by telehealth?

No. Some concerns require a physical examination, observations, testing, a procedure or another form of in-person assessment.

Telehealth service →

Is telehealth covered by Medicare?

Medicare eligibility depends on current rules, the appointment type and your individual circumstances. Reception can confirm the booking and billing arrangement before the consultation.

Telehealth information →

What happens if my practitioner decides I need an examination?

If the concern cannot be safely assessed remotely, you may be asked to attend the medical centre or another appropriate in-person service.

Referrals

How do referrals work?

A GP can assess whether a referral is appropriate and prepare one when clinically indicated. The referral should contain the information needed by the receiving specialist or service.

Referral information →

Do I need an appointment to discuss a referral?

A consultation may be required so the doctor can understand the reason for referral, review relevant information and make an appropriate clinical decision.

Referral information →

Can reception issue or renew a referral for me?

No. A referral is a clinical document and must be considered by a doctor. Reception can help you arrange the appropriate appointment.

Referral information →

After Hours Care

What should I do if I need medical care after the practice is closed?

When the practice is closed, patients who need after-hours medical assistance can contact Hello Home Doctor on 134 100. The After Hours Care page explains other available pathways.

After Hours Care →

What should I do in a medical emergency?

For a life-threatening illness or injury, call Triple Zero (000) or attend the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a routine appointment.

Emergency and after-hours information →

Can I call 13 HEALTH for advice?

Yes. Queenslanders can call 13 HEALTH on 13 43 25 84 for health assessment and advice from registered nurses, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

After Hours Care →

Healthcare Services

What healthcare services are available at the practice?

The practice provides general practice care across family health, women’s health, men’s health, children’s health, mental health, chronic disease care, preventive healthcare and telehealth, along with other services described on the Services page.

Our Services →

Do you provide chronic disease care?

Yes. The practice supports people living with long-term health conditions through ongoing general practice care, monitoring and coordinated planning.

Chronic Disease Care →

Can I discuss preventive healthcare with my GP?

Yes. Preventive healthcare can include health assessments, screening discussions, vaccination, cardiovascular risk review and lifestyle support based on your individual circumstances.

Preventive Healthcare →

How can I find a practitioner with an interest relevant to my care?

The Meet Our Team pages list verified practitioner qualifications, languages, areas of care and professional interests where publicly available. Reception can also help with practitioner availability.

Meet Our Team →

What if I am not sure which service I need?

You do not need to diagnose the problem yourself before booking. Reception can explain available appointment types, and your practitioner can assess your needs and discuss appropriate next steps.

Our Services →

Practice Information

Is parking available?

Patient parking is available nearby, including disability parking for patients who require it.

Access & Parking →

Is Woolloongabba Medical Centre wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The medical centre is designed for wheelchair access. Contact reception before attending if you have specific access or mobility needs.

Accessibility information →

How is my medical information protected?

Patient health information is treated as confidential and kept secure. Access is limited to authorised people who need the information for legitimate healthcare or practice purposes.

Confidentiality & Privacy →

Does the practice use reminders or recalls?

Yes. The practice uses reminder and recall processes to support preventive healthcare and clinically important follow-up.

Reminder & Recall Service →

How can I provide feedback or make a complaint?

You can raise feedback with the practice and ask to speak with the Practice Manager. If you prefer an external pathway, the Queensland Office of the Health Ombudsman accepts health-service complaints.

Feedback & Complaints →

Where can I read about practice policies, patient rights, quality and safety?
