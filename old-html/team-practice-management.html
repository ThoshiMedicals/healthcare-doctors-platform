Practice leadership

The systems behind dependable patient care.

Practice management supports the operational systems that allow a medical centre to function consistently, including quality, privacy, governance, workforce coordination and day-to-day practice operations.

Operational responsibility

Supporting a safe, accountable practice.

Our practice management team oversees patient feedback pathways and the systems that support quality, privacy, governance and day-to-day care. This connects patient experience with continuous improvement across the practice.

Healthcare Quality & Safety→Confidentiality & Privacy→Feedback & Concerns→Practice Policies→

How management fits the team

Good care depends on reliable systems as well as clinical decisions.

Practice management does not replace clinical judgement. Its role is to make sure the organisation around clinical care is dependable: staff responsibilities are clear, privacy and safety systems are maintained, patient feedback has a pathway, and operational issues are addressed rather than left disconnected from the patient experience.

Our approach

Transparency is part of trust.

We want patients to understand how we manage access, privacy, feedback, safety and everyday policies without having to search for basic information. The Practice Information centre brings those pathways together.
