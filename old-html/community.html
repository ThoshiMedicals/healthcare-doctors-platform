Healthcare with a sense of place

For many years, Woolloongabba Medical Centre has served individuals and families from communities throughout Brisbane’s inner south. We value the relationships we build locally and remain committed to providing accessible healthcare close to home.

Our Network

Healthcare across Brisbane communities

Our local commitment in Woolloongabba is part of a broader network of community-focused medical centres serving patients across Brisbane.

Patients also visit us from other nearby Brisbane suburbs for general practice, family healthcare and ongoing care.

Care that remains personal

People come to us from different streets and different stages of life, but the expectation is much the same: healthcare that feels local, personal and dependable. Our approach is built around continuity, prevention and long-term relationships.
