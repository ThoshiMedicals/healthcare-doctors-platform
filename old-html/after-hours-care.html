When we are open

During normal opening hours, contact reception on (07) 3217 3344 for appointment availability and advice about the most appropriate next step. For life-threatening illness or injury, call Triple Zero (000) rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

For a life-threatening illness or injury

Call Triple Zero (000) and ask for an ambulance. Queensland Health advises emergency care for symptoms such as severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, severe bleeding, major trauma or other life-threatening conditions.

Call 000

Hello Home Doctor

When the practice is closed, patients can contact Hello Home Doctor on 134 100 or use its online booking service. Hello Home Doctor is an accredited after-hours medical deputising service providing GP home visits and, where clinically appropriate, telehealth.

Published consultation hours: Monday to Friday 6:00 PM–8:00 AM; Saturday from midday; Sunday and public holidays 24 hours. Its call centre is open 24 hours to receive and triage requests.

Eligibility, service coverage, attendance times and bulk-billing conditions can vary. Hello Home Doctor advises patients to call 134 100 to confirm coverage and eligibility. This service is not for emergencies.

13 HEALTH

If you are unsure what level of care you need, Queenslanders can call 13 HEALTH — 13 43 25 84. Registered nurses provide health assessment and advice 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

After-hours questions

What should I do in a life-threatening emergency?

Call Triple Zero (000) and ask for an ambulance. Do not wait for a routine appointment when symptoms are life-threatening.

What after-hours service can I use when Woolloongabba Medical Centre is closed?

When we are closed, contact Hello Home Doctor on 134 100 or use its online booking service. It provides after-hours GP home visits and, where clinically appropriate, telehealth. Eligibility and coverage conditions apply.

What if I am unsure which after-hours option is appropriate?

If you are unsure what level of care you need, Queenslanders can call 13 HEALTH on 13 43 25 84 for health assessment and advice from registered nurses, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
