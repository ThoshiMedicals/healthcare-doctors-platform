Professional background

About Dr Sambo Luon

Dr Sambo Luon completed his medical degree at Monash University in 2010. His Healthengine profile records hospital experience in Sydney, emergency-department work in Moree during general practice training, and Fellowship of the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners in 2017. The practice lists preventive medicine as his professional focus.
