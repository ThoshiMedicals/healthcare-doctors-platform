Professional background

About Dr Anand Singh

Dr Anand Singh is an experienced General Practitioner whose public profile notes postgraduate study in health and hospital management and in maternal and child health. His stated areas of interest include dermatology, women’s health and geriatric medicine, and the practice also lists child health among his areas of care.
