Professional background

About Dr Nirupa Sivathasan

Dr Nirupa Sivathasan is a British-born and qualified General Practitioner who has worked in London. After moving to Australia in 2016, her public profile records a year in acute medicine, including cardiology and hospital-in-the-home work, before she returned to primary care. Her profile describes a holistic approach to reviewing patients.
