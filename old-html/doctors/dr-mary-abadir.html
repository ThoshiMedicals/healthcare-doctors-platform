Professional background

About Dr Mary Abadir

Dr Mary Abadir is a specialist obstetrician and gynaecologist. Her public profile records MRCOG membership since 2002 and FRANZCOG membership since 2017, together with more than 20 years of experience in obstetrics. She provides specialist care across pregnancy and gynaecology for women at different life stages.
