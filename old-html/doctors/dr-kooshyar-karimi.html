Professional background

About Dr Kooshyar Karimi

Dr Kooshyar Karimi is a specialist General Practitioner who has practised in Australia since 2002. His public profile records Fellowship of the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners in 2011 and membership of the Australian and New Zealand Skin Cancer Society and the Australian Society of Cosmetic Medicine.
