Professional background

About Dr Gemunu Suresh Ranawake

Dr Gemunu Suresh Ranawake completed his medical training at the University of Ruhuna in Sri Lanka and undertook internship experience in paediatrics, obstetrics and gynaecology. His public profile records general practice work from 2008 and previous medical officer experience in anaesthesiology, emergency medicine and critical care.
