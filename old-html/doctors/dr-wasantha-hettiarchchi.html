Professional background

About Dr Wasantha Hettiarchchi

Dr Wasantha Hettiarchchi graduated from the University of Colombo, Sri Lanka, in 1982 and has long-standing experience in general practice. His public profile identifies diabetes management as a particular interest and lists a broad range of other areas of care.
