Our philosophy of care

Good care begins with understanding the person.

Health changes with age, family, work and circumstance. The more we understand that wider story, the more thoughtful care can become.

We value continuity because trust takes time. Familiarity gives each conversation context and allows care to grow with the person, rather than begin again at every appointment.

Read our philosophy of care

Our story

A local practice with a long view of care.

Woolloongabba Medical Centre exists to give local patients a dependable place to return to — not only when something is wrong, but through the ordinary changes and milestones that shape a lifetime of health.

Our role in the community is simple: to remain present, to know our patients well and to provide care with enough continuity that relationships can deepen over time.

Accreditation reflects an ongoing commitment to quality and safety.

Our community

Healthcare with a sense of place.

Our practice is part of the everyday life of Woolloongabba and the neighbouring communities of Kangaroo Point, East Brisbane, South Brisbane, Greenslopes, Stones Corner and Dutton Park.

People come to us from different streets and different stages of life, but the expectation is much the same: care that feels local, personal and dependable.
