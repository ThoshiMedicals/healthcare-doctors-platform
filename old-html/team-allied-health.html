Allied Health

Support that can extend beyond the GP consultation.

Allied health can complement general practice when a patient needs additional support with a specific health goal. The practice’s current public practitioner directory includes chiropractic and physiotherapy. Practitioner availability and referral requirements can change, so reception can confirm the most appropriate pathway when you book.

Coordinated healthcare

Working alongside medical care.

Allied health can support rehabilitation, movement and other defined healthcare goals. Where appropriate, allied health can work alongside GP care so that the patient’s wider health context, referrals and follow-up remain connected rather than becoming separate pathways.
