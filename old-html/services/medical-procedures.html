What is this service?

Procedures and medical assessments within general practice.

Some healthcare needs can be assessed, monitored or treated within the medical centre rather than in hospital. The exact service available depends on your clinical needs, the practitioner, appointment type and any equipment or preparation required.

Why does it matter?

Clear preparation makes procedural care safer and more efficient.

Many procedures require a clinical assessment first. Some need recent pathology, consent, specific forms, medication planning or a longer appointment. Checking these requirements before you attend helps the practice prepare appropriately.

How can we help?

Procedures and assessments

  • Iron Infusions Assessment, preparation, the infusion visit and follow-up.
  • Skin Checks Assessment of moles, spots and skin changes.
  • Skin Cancer Care Assessment, biopsy/excision pathways and follow-up.
  • Breast Check Clinical assessment of breast changes and screening questions.
  • Prostate Care Urinary symptoms, prostate health and PSA discussions.
  • Ear Care & Ear Syringing Assessment of blocked ears and irrigation where appropriate.
  • Wound Dressings Wound assessment, dressings and planned follow-up.
  • ECG Resting electrocardiogram and clinical follow-up.
  • Spirometry Lung-function testing and interpretation in clinical context.
  • Nebulising Nebulised medicines when clinically appropriate.
  • Implanon Insertion & Removal Contraceptive implant consultation, procedure and aftercare.
  • IUD Insertion & Removal Intrauterine contraception assessment, procedure and follow-up.
  • Pap Smear & Cervical Screening Current Cervical Screening Test information and follow-up.
  • Antenatal & Postnatal Care Pregnancy and after-birth GP care and coordination.
  • Pre-Pregnancy Care Preconception health, medicines, vaccination and planning.
  • Commercial & Rideshare Driver Medicals. Medical assessments can be arranged where the appropriate clinician and appointment are available. Bring required forms or licensing documentation.
  • Cryotherapy. Freezing treatment may be used for selected skin lesions when a clinician considers it appropriate.
Fees may apply.

Contact reception for pricing details. The team can also confirm whether you need a standard consultation, longer appointment or separate procedure booking. A WorkCover or work injury medical is arranged with reception before you attend. Insurance medicals and insurance forms are private-fee requests; ask reception to confirm the fee and the paperwork to bring.

Planning your appointment

Booking, preparation, follow-up and costs.

How to book

For procedures, call reception before booking so the team can confirm whether you need an assessment first, which clinician performs the procedure, and how much time is required.

Before your appointment

Preparation is procedure-specific. Use the relevant procedure page below and follow any instructions provided by the treating clinician or reception team.

After your appointment & follow-up

Aftercare varies by procedure. You will be given instructions about wound care, results, warning signs and any review appointment that is needed.

Costs

Standard Medicare-eligible GP consultations are 100% bulk billed at Woolloongabba Medical Centre for patients with a valid Medicare card. Procedures, consumables, medicines or devices, reports and other non-Medicare services may attract additional fees. Reception will confirm the current practice charge before treatment or booking wherever practical. External providers set and bill their own fees.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak with reception before booking a procedure?

Yes. Contact reception so the team can confirm whether the service is available, the appropriate appointment type, any preparation required and whether a fee applies.

Are medical procedures bulk billed?

Some procedures, consumables, medical assessments and non-Medicare services may attract a fee. Contact reception for current pricing details before booking.

Can every procedure be done at the first appointment?

Not always. A clinician may need to assess you first, review results, discuss options, obtain consent or arrange a separate procedure appointment.

What should I bring for a driver or employment medical?

Bring the relevant form or employer/licensing paperwork, photo identification and any reports or information requested for the assessment. For a WorkCover or work injury medical, bring the workplace form as well.

What if I need urgent medical care?

Procedures and routine assessments are not emergency services. For a life-threatening emergency call Triple Zero (000).
