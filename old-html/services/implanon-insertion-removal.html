Procedure information

What this care involves.

Implanon NXT is the contraceptive implant available in Australia. It releases a progestogen hormone and can provide long-acting contraception. Insertion and removal must be performed by a clinician trained in the procedure, after an appropriate contraception consultation.

What happens during the appointment?

For insertion, local anaesthetic is used to numb the skin before the implant is placed under the skin of the upper arm. Removal also uses local anaesthetic and a small skin opening. A new implant can sometimes be inserted at the same appointment if clinically appropriate.

This page provides general information only. The treating clinician will explain what is appropriate for your individual circumstances and obtain consent where a procedure is planned.

Before your appointment

How to book and prepare.

How to book

Call reception before booking. The team will confirm trained-practitioner availability and whether you need an initial contraception consultation before the procedure appointment. This helps ensure timing, suitability, prescription and consent are addressed first.

Book AppointmentCall (07) 3217 3344

Before your appointment

Bring your current medicines list and contraception history. Tell the clinician if pregnancy is possible, if you have unexplained bleeding, or if you have had problems with hormonal contraception. Do not stop or change contraception unless your clinician advises you to do so.

After your appointment

Follow-up and aftercare.

You will receive instructions about the dressing and wound care, when the implant becomes effective, and what symptoms should prompt review. Contact the practice if you are concerned about the wound, cannot feel the implant as expected, or develop symptoms that worry you.

Costs

Know about fees before you attend.

Exact additional fees are confirmed before treatment.

The standard Medicare-eligible GP consultation is bulk billed in line with the practice billing policy. Implanon insertion or removal may involve an additional procedure fee, device or medicine cost, local anaesthetic and consumables. Reception will confirm the current practice charge before the procedure is booked wherever practical. Pharmacy charges are separate and are set by the pharmacy.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I have the implant inserted at my first appointment?

Not always. An initial consultation may be needed to review suitability, timing, consent and prescription requirements before the procedure.

Who can insert or remove Implanon?

Insertion and removal must be performed by a trained clinician. Reception can confirm current availability.

Can a new implant be inserted when the old one is removed?

This may be possible when clinically appropriate. Discuss your contraception plan with the treating clinician before the procedure.

Will I need follow-up?

Your clinician will explain the expected wound care, contraceptive timing and when to return if there are concerns.
