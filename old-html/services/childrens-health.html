What is this service?

Healthcare that grows with your child.

Children’s health in general practice includes care for common childhood illness, preventive healthcare, immunisation, growth and development concerns, and health issues that arise through childhood and adolescence.

A GP can assess a concern, review the child’s history, provide treatment where appropriate and arrange further investigation or referral when needed.

Why does it matter?

Children’s health can change quickly as they grow.

Development, immunisation, school, family circumstances and changing health needs all shape a child’s care. A regular practice can help families keep a consistent record and recognise changes over time.

Continuity can be particularly useful when a child has recurrent symptoms, ongoing medicines or input from several health professionals.

How can we help?

Care for illness, development and prevention.

Depending on your child’s needs, care may include:

  • Assessment of common childhood illness
  • Childhood immunisation
  • Growth and developmental concerns
  • Preventive healthcare and health advice
  • Skin, allergy or respiratory concerns within general practice scope
  • Adolescent health discussions
  • Ongoing management of long-term health conditions
  • Referral for paediatric, allied health or other specialist assessment when clinically appropriate

Understanding your care

Preparing for a child’s appointment.

Bring relevant child health or vaccination records, medication information and any recent reports that may help the consultation. For development or recurrent symptoms, notes about what you have observed can also be useful.

If the appointment involves several concerns, forms or a detailed developmental history, ask reception whether additional consultation time is appropriate.

This information is general in nature. Your practitioner can provide advice based on your individual health history and circumstances.

What should I do next?

Book Appointment and bring the information that helps us understand your child.

Book a children’s health appointment when your child needs assessment, preventive care or follow-up. Reception can help with appointment length and practitioner availability.

  • Bring the child’s Medicare details and relevant health or vaccination records.
  • Bring a current medication list and recent reports where relevant.
  • Ask for a longer appointment if several issues need to be discussed.
  • For severe breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness or another life-threatening emergency, call 000.

Planning your appointment

Booking, preparation, follow-up and costs.

How to book

Book a GP appointment for your child. Tell reception if your child is very young, acutely unwell, needs vaccination, or may need a longer appointment.

Before your appointment

Bring the child’s Medicare details, medicines, relevant discharge or specialist information, and immunisation history if it may be relevant.

After your appointment & follow-up

Follow-up may include results, a repeat examination, vaccination, referral or monitoring. The practice may use HotDoc for reminders, forms or result-related communication where appropriate.

Costs

Standard Medicare-eligible GP consultations are 100% bulk billed at Woolloongabba Medical Centre for patients with a valid Medicare card. Procedures, consumables, medicines or devices, reports and other non-Medicare services may attract additional fees. Reception will confirm the current practice charge before treatment or booking wherever practical. External providers set and bill their own fees.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide childhood vaccinations?

Yes. Childhood immunisation services are available in accordance with current Australian immunisation recommendations and individual clinical circumstances.

Can I discuss growth or development concerns with a GP?

Yes. A GP can review the concern, consider the child’s history and development, and arrange further assessment or referral if needed.

What should I bring to a child’s appointment?

Bring the child’s Medicare details, relevant vaccination or child health records, medication information and any recent reports that may help the consultation.

Do you provide school or sports medicals?

Yes. We provide general healthcare for children and adolescents. Contact reception to confirm the appointment type and any forms that need to be completed.
