Service information

Accessing radiology services.

Diagnostic imaging can include different types of scans and imaging tests used to investigate symptoms, injuries or medical conditions. The modalities available on site, appointment requirements and reporting arrangements are determined by the independent radiology provider.

Your GP or specialist can discuss whether imaging is appropriate and provide a referral when required.

Independent provider. This service is provided by a separate third-party business and is not owned or operated by Woolloongabba Medical Centre. Provider availability, opening hours, service scope, fees and billing can change independently of the medical centre.

Before you attend

How to access the service and prepare.

How to access radiology

Bring the imaging referral supplied by your clinician. Contact the radiology provider to confirm whether the requested imaging is available on site and whether you need an appointment.

Before imaging

Preparation depends on the test. Some imaging may require fasting, drinking water, a full bladder, removal of metal items or specific instructions about medicines or contrast. Follow the instructions provided by the radiology service and tell them about pregnancy, allergies, kidney problems or implanted devices where relevant.

After the service

Results, medicines and follow-up.

The radiology provider prepares the report and normally sends it to the clinician who requested the imaging. Arrange follow-up with the referring clinician as advised so the report can be interpreted together with your symptoms, examination and other tests.

Costs

Know who is responsible for fees.

Third-party provider fees are separate.

Radiology fees are set and billed by the independent imaging provider. Medicare may cover some or all of the cost of eligible diagnostic imaging, depending on the test, referral and Medicare rules. Ask the radiology provider about its current fee and any out-of-pocket amount before the test. A GP consultation at Woolloongabba Medical Centre is billed separately under the medical centre billing policy.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?

Many diagnostic imaging services require a referral from a GP or specialist. Bring the referral and confirm the provider requirements when making the imaging booking.

Is every type of scan available on site?

Not necessarily. Available imaging types, equipment and appointment times are controlled by the independent radiology provider. Confirm availability directly with the provider.

Who explains the imaging report?

The clinician who requested the imaging is usually best placed to explain the report and what it means for your care. Book follow-up as advised.

Is radiology operated by the medical centre?

No. The on-site radiology service is an independent third-party provider. Its clinical services, staffing, bookings, fees and billing are separate from Woolloongabba Medical Centre.
