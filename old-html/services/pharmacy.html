Service information

Accessing pharmacy services.

The independent pharmacy can assist with dispensing valid prescriptions, medicine supply and pharmacist advice within the pharmacy scope of practice. The pharmacy controls its own opening hours, stock, dispensing processes and professional services.

Your Woolloongabba Medical Centre clinician remains responsible for medical prescribing decisions made during your consultation.

Independent provider. This service is provided by a separate third-party business and is not owned or operated by Woolloongabba Medical Centre. Provider availability, opening hours, service scope, fees and billing can change independently of the medical centre.

Before you attend

How to access the service and prepare.

How to access the pharmacy

Take your paper prescription or electronic prescription token to the pharmacy. Bring your Medicare card and any relevant concession, DVA or safety-net information if applicable.

Before collecting medicines

Tell the pharmacist about allergies and other medicines or supplements you use, especially if they are not already known. If a medicine is unavailable or there is a prescribing query, the pharmacist may contact the prescriber or discuss suitable next steps with you.

After the service

Results, medicines and follow-up.

Follow the directions provided by your prescriber and pharmacist. If you experience a possible medicine reaction, have questions about ongoing treatment or need a medication review, contact the medical centre or seek urgent care when appropriate. Do not stop or change a prescribed medicine without professional advice unless you have been told to do so.

Costs

Know who is responsible for fees.

Third-party provider fees are separate.

Prescription, medicine and pharmacy service charges are set and billed by the independent pharmacy. PBS subsidies, concession arrangements and patient contributions apply according to current Australian rules and the medicine supplied. Woolloongabba Medical Centre does not set or receive pharmacy charges. Medical-centre consultation fees are separate.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the pharmacy dispense an eScript?

Electronic prescription dispensing is subject to the pharmacy systems and a valid electronic prescription token. Present the token to the pharmacy and ask the pharmacist if you need assistance.

Are pharmacy fees included in my GP appointment?

No. The pharmacy is an independent business. Medicine and pharmacy service charges are separate from Woolloongabba Medical Centre billing.

What if the pharmacy does not have my medicine?

The pharmacist can explain stock or ordering options and may contact the prescriber if a medicine-related clarification is needed. Do not substitute or change treatment without appropriate professional advice.

Is the pharmacy owned by Woolloongabba Medical Centre?

No. The on-site pharmacy is independently owned and operated. Its opening hours, stock, professional services, prices and billing are controlled by the pharmacy.
