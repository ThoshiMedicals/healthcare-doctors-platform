What is this service?

Vaccination is preventive healthcare tailored to the individual.

Vaccinations help reduce the risk of some infectious diseases and their complications. The vaccines that may be appropriate for you can depend on age, health conditions, pregnancy, previous vaccination history, occupation, travel plans and other risk factors.

A GP or clinical team member can review your history, discuss suitability and timing, and help plan routine, catch-up or travel-related vaccination care.

Why does it matter?

Vaccination protects individuals and supports community health.

Staying appropriately vaccinated can lower the chance of becoming seriously unwell from vaccine-preventable diseases. It is also an important part of preventive care for children, adults, older people and people with particular health or occupational risks.

Vaccination needs change over time, which is why a clinical review is useful when records are incomplete, health circumstances change or travel is planned.

How can we help?

Support for routine, catch-up and travel-related vaccination care.

Depending on your needs and clinical assessment, care may include:

  • Routine childhood and adolescent immunisation discussions
  • Adult vaccination and preventive-care review
  • Influenza vaccination
  • Catch-up vaccination when previous doses may have been missed
  • Travel-health vaccination planning where clinically appropriate
  • Review of vaccination history, timing and individual suitability
  • Advice about expected common reactions and when to seek medical attention
  • Reminder and recall support where appropriate

Understanding your care

Bring your vaccination history if you have it.

A vaccination record, immunisation history statement or information about previous doses can help your practitioner assess what may be needed. If your history is incomplete, do not guess—book a review so the available information can be assessed.

Travel vaccination planning is best started before departure because recommended vaccines and timing depend on destination, itinerary, health history and how soon you are travelling. Vaccine availability can change, so reception can confirm practical arrangements when you book.

This information is general in nature. Your practitioner can provide advice based on your individual health history and circumstances.

What should I do next?

Book a vaccination review or ask reception which appointment is appropriate.

  • Bring your Medicare card and any available vaccination records.
  • Tell the practitioner about allergies, medicines, pregnancy or significant health conditions.
  • For travel health, bring your destination, departure date and itinerary details.
  • If you are acutely unwell, contact the practice before attending so the clinical team can advise on the most appropriate next step.

Travel health

Travel vaccination planning

Travel vaccination needs depend on your destination, itinerary, timing, medical history and previous vaccines. Book early where possible and bring any available vaccination records. Your clinician can discuss which vaccines or other travel-health advice may be appropriate for you.

Some travel vaccines or non-Medicare services may attract a private fee. Contact reception before booking if you need current pricing or vaccine availability.

Planning your appointment

Booking, preparation, follow-up and costs.

How to book

Book through reception or HotDoc as available. Tell the team which vaccine you are seeking so stock, eligibility and appointment type can be confirmed.

Before your appointment

Bring any vaccination record that may not already be available and tell the clinician about previous vaccine reactions, pregnancy or relevant medical conditions.

After your appointment & follow-up

You may be asked to remain for observation after vaccination. The clinician will explain expected reactions, when another dose is due and when to seek medical advice.

Costs

Standard Medicare-eligible GP consultations are 100% bulk billed at Woolloongabba Medical Centre for patients with a valid Medicare card. Procedures, consumables, medicines or devices, reports and other non-Medicare services may attract additional fees. Reception will confirm the current practice charge before treatment or booking wherever practical. External providers set and bill their own fees.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide vaccinations for children and adults?

The practice provides vaccination care across different ages. The vaccines that are appropriate depend on the person’s age, health, previous vaccination history and current Australian recommendations.

What if my vaccination history is incomplete?

Book a review and bring any records you can find. Your practitioner can assess the available information and discuss whether catch-up vaccination may be appropriate rather than relying on guesswork.

When should I arrange travel vaccinations?

Plan as early as practical before travel. Recommendations and timing vary by destination, itinerary, health history and the vaccines involved. Reception can help arrange an appropriate travel-health appointment.

Can I be vaccinated if I am pregnant, taking medicines or have a medical condition?

Suitability depends on the vaccine and your individual circumstances. Tell your practitioner about pregnancy, medicines, allergies and relevant health conditions so they can assess what is appropriate.

Do I need to check vaccine availability before attending?

Availability can vary. If you are booking for a particular vaccine or travel requirement, contact reception so the practice can confirm the current arrangements.
