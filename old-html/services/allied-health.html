What is this service?

Allied health as part of connected care.

Allied health practitioners bring additional expertise that can complement general practice. At Woolloongabba Medical Centre, current verified practitioner profiles include chiropractic and physiotherapy.

Why does it matter?

Different disciplines can contribute to one care plan.

Musculoskeletal pain, mobility, rehabilitation and functional concerns can benefit from coordinated care. When relevant and with appropriate consent, your GP and allied health practitioner can work from a shared understanding of your health needs.

How can we help?

Current allied health disciplines

  • Chiropractic care. Dr Steven Chang’s verified profile includes manual and lower-force chiropractic approaches, instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilisation, dry needling and trigger point therapy.
  • Physiotherapy. Mr Samuel Stafford is listed as a physiotherapist in the current practitioner directory.

Planning your appointment

Booking, preparation, follow-up and costs.

How to book

Book through reception or the relevant allied health booking pathway. A GP referral is not always required, but some Medicare or care-plan pathways do require one.

Before your appointment

Bring relevant imaging, reports, referrals and a medicines list if they relate to the problem being treated.

After your appointment & follow-up

Your allied health clinician will explain the treatment plan, home exercises or review schedule. If GP review or another referral is needed, this will be discussed.

Costs

Standard Medicare-eligible GP consultations are 100% bulk billed at Woolloongabba Medical Centre for patients with a valid Medicare card. Procedures, consumables, medicines or devices, reports and other non-Medicare services may attract additional fees. Reception will confirm the current practice charge before treatment or booking wherever practical. External providers set and bill their own fees.

Patient questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What allied health services are represented at the practice?

Current practitioner profiles include chiropractic and physiotherapy. Practitioner availability and appointment arrangements can change.

Do I need a GP referral?

Referral requirements depend on the service, your funding arrangement and the reason for care. Contact reception or the practitioner booking service if you are unsure.

Can allied health work alongside my GP care?

Yes. With appropriate consent and information sharing, allied health and general practice can support a coordinated approach to musculoskeletal, rehabilitation and ongoing health needs.

How do I book?

Use the relevant practitioner profile for current booking options or contact reception for administrative guidance.
