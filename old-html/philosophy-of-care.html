Healthcare built on relationships

We believe healthcare is more than treating illness. Healthcare works best when practitioners understand the person behind the appointment and can bring context from previous care into the next conversation.

Strong healthcare relationships support continuity, greater understanding and more personalised care over time.

What guides our care

  • Trust
  • Continuity
  • Connection
  • Community
  • Long-term wellbeing

Our approach also values holistic care, health promotion, disease prevention, dignity and respect for every patient.

Healthcare with continuity

Patients often choose a healthcare practice because of convenience. Many stay because of relationships. At Woolloongabba Medical Centre, we believe continuity of care creates a more connected patient experience because a person’s history, goals and circumstances can be understood over time.

Our practice serves Woolloongabba and surrounding Brisbane communities, including East Brisbane, Kangaroo Point, South Brisbane, Greenslopes, Stones Corner and Dutton Park.

Quality and safety

Accreditation reflects an ongoing commitment to quality and safety standards in Australian general practice.
