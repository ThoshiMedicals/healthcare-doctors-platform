5. How to class a defect

ClassUse it whenExamples on this platform
CriticalA staff computer cannot use the site, a patient could be sent to the wrong place, or unpublished clinic information is public.The network address does not load. HotDoc shows another clinic. The phone, address, or hours are wrong. A draft is visible on the public page. Sign-in fails for Clinic Admin.
HighAn approved fact about a doctor or the practice is wrong, or a required sign-off cannot be done.A doctor’s name, photograph, or biography does not match the approved profile. The wrong doctor has Call Reception. No Clinic Admin account exists. Publish is offered on a draft.
MediumThe task can be finished, but a label, search result, or path is misleading.Search does not find a doctor who is on the team page. A menu name does not match the page staff expected. The revision list is hard to understand.
LowThe fact and the task are correct. The note is a preference.A sentence could be shorter. Spacing feels tight. A doctor would prefer a different photograph later.

A missing portrait for Dr Nirupa Sivathasan or Mr Samuel Stafford is not a defect. A wish for a new page, a new image region, or a new CMS field is not a defect. Record it as out of scope and do not build it in this test.
